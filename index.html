HOW TO PLAY

🔴 HP — death returns you to Grace. 🟢 Stamina — attacks, rolls & sprint drain it. 🔵 FP — focus for charged heavies (hold ATK).

◈ Farm runes from Hollows. Rest at golden Sites of Grace to refill flasks, respawn foes, and level up Vigor / Strength / Endurance.

⚔ 3-hit combo • hold ATK for heavy • roll has i-frames • lock-on keeps boss centered • heal with ⚱ flasks (5).
